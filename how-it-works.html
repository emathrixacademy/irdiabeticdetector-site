How the station works

DFU Thermography Screening Station · eMathrix Technologies

Research instrument, not a medical device. The station does not diagnose or grade anyone. Wagner grades come only from a licensed clinician. The colour model's output is a class, never a grade.

Contents: 1. How the machine learning works · 2. Program flowchart · 3. Exploded parts of the device

1. How the machine learning works

The colour model is MobileNetV2. It is trained on a separate computer, never on the station, and only copied to the station as a finished file. The 2.2 °C left–right temperature check is not machine learning: it is fixed arithmetic and is never trained.

Today: the stand-in model is trained on the Mermer, Aksu et al. Raspberry Pi dataset (grades 0–2), because the station has no graded scans of its own yet. It is to be retrained on station scans graded by the study clinician, after ethics approval. Accuracy from public data is reported with its limits: the images are burst shots of few patients, so they are grouped by capture session before any split.
