DFU Thermography Screening Station

Built by eMathrix Technologies · Client acceptance: October 30, 2026

Research instrument, not a medical device. The station does not diagnose, grade or treat anyone. Wagner grades come only from a licensed clinician.

The station runs fully offline on the Raspberry Pi 5. Scans stay on the station's own storage and nothing is sent anywhere. Copying data off the station is done by the research team under their own data procedures. Never upload scans or session files to any online service.
